Centurion University GPS-Free Positioning Policy

Indoor spaces at Centurion University of Technology and Management utilize localized Bluetooth Low Energy transmitters, Wi-Fi 802.11mc Round-Trip Time triangulation, and IMU inertial dead-reckoning. Satellite GPS reception is intentionally not required, ensuring zero battery drain and continuous offline availability.
